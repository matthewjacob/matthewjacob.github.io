Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy (Economics Track) at the Harvard Kennedy School. My research interests are in public and labor economics, with a focus on housing, intergenerational mobility and social capital.

I am an [NBER Graduate Fellow](https://www.nber.org/programs-projects/projects-and-centers/fellowships-consumer-financial-management/graduate-student-fellowships-consumer-financial-management-fellows) in Consumer Financial Management, a [Meyer Fellow](https://www.jchs.harvard.edu/previous-meyer-fellows) at the Harvard Joint Center for Housing Studies, a Contingent Worker in the [Central Applied Science](https://research.facebook.com/teams/cas/) team at Meta, and a Graduate Affiliate at [Opportunity Insights](https://opportunityinsights.org/).
